travel-sync

A private tool that syncs one person's travel itineraries between their own Google Drive folder and their own home server. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

travel-sync is used by its owner only. It accesses one Google Drive folder belonging to the owner to copy travel itinerary files to and from the owner's private home server.

No data is collected from anyone else, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's server.
